# Lapida Token — Tokenomics & Distribution (BNB Chain)

---

## 1. Overview

Lapida Token employs a multi-token system for platform payments, governance, and utility:  
- **LPD:** Stablecoin, reserve-backed, used for payments and donations.  
- **MLPD:** Utility token, fixed supply, for farming, staking, platform economics.  
- **GLPD:** Governance token, used for DAO voting, proposals, delegation.

---

## 2. Token Details

| Token  | Ticker | Standard | Supply         | Use Cases           |
|--------|--------|----------|---------------|---------------------|
| LPD    | LPD    | BEP-20   | Dynamic (mint/burn) | Payments, donations |
| MLPD   | MLPD   | BEP-20   | 100,000,000   | Farming, staking, rewards |
| GLPD   | GLPD   | BEP-20   | 10,000,000    | DAO voting, governance |

---

## 3. Initial Distribution

### LPD (Stablecoin)
- Minted/burned as needed, backed by platform reserves (USDT, USDC, BNB)
- Reserve management via multisig and DAO

### MLPD (Utility)
- **Farming/Staking rewards:** 60%
- **Team & Advisors (vesting):** 15%
- **Ecosystem & Partnerships:** 10%
- **IDO/Pre-sale:** 10%
- **Reserve:** 5%

### GLPD (Governance)
- **DAO Treasury:** 60%
- **Team (vesting):** 20%
- **Ecosystem/Community:** 10%
- **IDO/Pre-sale:** 10%

---

## 4. Vesting & Unlock Schedules

- Team/advisor allocations subject to 12-24 month vesting, cliff periods, linear unlock.
- IDO/Pre-sale tokens: instant or short-term unlock as per round rules.
- All schedules published on [lapida.one](https://lapida.one) and visible via dashboard and BNB Chain explorer.

---

## 5. Reserve Management

- LPD mint/burn strictly controlled by ReserveContract.
- Multi-currency reserves (USDT, USDC, BNB) held in audited, multisig wallets.
- Transparency: all reserve transactions public on BNB Chain and dashboard.

---

## 6. Utility & Rewards

- MLPD for farming, staking, platform rewards, and internal transactions.
- GLPD for governance, voting, proposal creation, delegation.
- LPD for payments, donations, memorial management.

---

## 7. Token Buyback/Burn (Planned)

- Platform fees may be used for periodic buyback and burn (deflationary effect).
- Decisions via DAO vote, transparency in all actions.
