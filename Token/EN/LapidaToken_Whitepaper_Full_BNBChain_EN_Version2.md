# Lapida Token — Full Whitepaper (BNB Chain, EN)

---

## 1. Introduction

Lapida Token is a decentralized platform built on BNB Chain for memorial management, charitable donations, and community governance through DAO.  
The project combines a stablecoin, utility token, DAO mechanisms, and NFT memorials, providing transparency, security, and scalability.

---

## 2. Vision & Mission

**Project goal:** To create a secure, transparent, and scalable platform for digital memorials, donations, and community-driven governance.  
Lapida aims to bridge traditional memorial services with Web3 technologies, supporting memory preservation, family support, and charitable initiatives.

---

## 3. Platform Architecture

### 3.1. Multi-token Model

- **LPD:** Stablecoin, reserve-backed (USDT, USDC, BNB), used for payments and donations.
- **MLPD:** Utility token (fixed supply), designed for farming, staking, and platform economics.
- **GLPD:** Governance token, used for voting, proposal creation, and delegation in the DAO.

### 3.2. Smart Contracts

- **ReserveContract:** Manages reserves and controls LPD minting/burning.
- **VestingContract:** Handles vesting for team, investors, advisors — linear and cliff schedules.
- **DAO Contracts:** Governor, Timelock, ProposalFactory — voting, proposals, security.
- **Sale Contracts:** IDO/Pre-sale, public sale, DEX listing.
- **NFT Contracts:** ERC-721/1155 for digital memorials, integrations with external NFT platforms.

### 3.3. Integration Layer

- API (REST, GraphQL), Web3 interfaces for wallets, DEX, external services.
- Support for WalletConnect, MetaMask, TrustWallet, Binance Chain Wallet.

---

## 4. Security & Audit

- OpenZeppelin libraries, reentrancy protection, SafeMath, access controls.
- Multisig and timelock for critical actions (reserves, upgrades).
- Internal and external audits (SOLIDproof, OpenZeppelin, Hacken).
- Public reports and transaction transparency via BNB Chain explorer and dashboard.

---

## 5. DAO & Governance

- All major decisions are made via DAO: upgrades, partnerships, reserve management, technical changes.
- On-platform voting, delegation, proposal discussions before voting.
- Timelock: delays execution for added security.
- Multisig: trusted parties control reserves and emergency actions.

---

## 6. Tokenomics & Distribution

- **LPD:** Mint/burn as needed, backed by reserves, transparent wallets.
- **MLPD:** 60% farming/staking, 15% team/advisors (vesting), 10% ecosystem, 10% IDO/Pre-sale, 5% reserve.
- **GLPD:** 60% DAO treasury, 20% team (vesting), 10% ecosystem, 10% IDO/Pre-sale.
- All vesting schedules and allocations published on the website and dashboard.

---

## 7. Memorial NFTs

- Create, manage, and transfer digital memorials (NFTs) — memory preservation, family history, donations.
- Integrations with TofuNFT, OpenSea, internal NFT marketplace.
- Ability to link NFTs to donations, events, biographies.
