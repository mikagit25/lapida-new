# Lapida Token — Marketing & Community Plan (BNB Chain)

---

## 1. Introduction

Successful development of Lapida Token requires active community support, transparent communication, quality marketing, and strong partnerships.  
The goal is to build trust, expand the audience, attract investors, and ensure sustainable platform growth.

---

## 2. Target Audience

- Blockchain and DeFi investors
- Partner services (ritual companies, memorial platforms)
- Web3, DAO, NFT enthusiasts
- Developers and integrators
- Media, bloggers, crypto industry influencers

## 4. Launch Strategy

### 4.1. Pre-sale / IDO

- Announcements on social media and industry channels
- Partner publications with PinkSale, PancakeSwap
- AMA sessions on Telegram/YouTube
- Engaging influencers

### 4.2. Post-IDO

- Contests, airdrops for active participants
- Launching a referral program
- Publishing token distribution reports
- Regular AMAs and educational materials

---

## 5. Community Development Plan

- **DAO voting and polls**
- Monthly reports and roadmaps
- Ambassador and influencer programs
- Community suggestion pool (feature requests, integrations)
- Bug bounty program for developers

---

## 6. PR, Articles & Partner Integrations

- Publications on leading resources: ForkLog, Cointelegraph, Crypto.ru, etc.
- Press releases about launches/listings/partnerships
- Integrations with memorial NFT services, DeFi platforms, DEX
- Participation in offline/online conferences

---

## 7. KPI & Success Metrics

- Number of members in Telegram, Twitter, Discord
- Trading volume and liquidity on DEX
- Number of active DAO voters
- Successful integrations with external services
- Community engagement level (polls, suggestions, bug reports)

---

## 8. Risks & Response Measures

- **Toxicity and FUD:** prompt moderation, transparent answers
- **Low interest:** contests, airdrops, partner activities
- **Low DAO activity:** incentivized rewards, user education
- **Security:** constant channel monitoring, phishing protection
