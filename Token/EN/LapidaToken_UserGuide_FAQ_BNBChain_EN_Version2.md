# Lapida Token — User Guide & FAQ (BNB Chain)

---

## 1. Introduction

Lapida Token is a multi-token ecosystem for managing digital memorials, funding, and DAO participation on the BNB Chain.  
This document provides main user scenarios, step-by-step instructions, and answers to frequently asked questions.

---

## 2. How to Buy Lapida Tokens (LPD, MLPD, GLPD)?

### 2.1. Through Public Sale (IDO/Pre-sale)
- Go to: [lapida.one](https://lapida.one)
- Register using a Web3 wallet (MetaMask, TrustWallet, Binance Chain Wallet)
- Follow instructions to participate in IDO/Pre-sale:
  1. Connect your wallet
  2. Select the desired token (LPD, MLPD, GLPD)
  3. Enter the amount and confirm the transaction
  4. Receive tokens in your wallet after the sale is completed

### 2.2. On DEX (PancakeSwap)
- Go to [PancakeSwap](https://pancakeswap.finance)
- Import the token address (listed on lapida.one after launch)
- Swap BNB/USDT/USDC for LPD, MLPD, or GLPD

---

## 3. How to Participate in DAO and Voting?

- Hold GLPD tokens in your wallet.
- Go to the DAO section on [lapida.one](https://lapida.one)
- Review current proposals.
- Vote "For" or "Against" on relevant issues.
- Use the "Delegate" function to delegate your votes — specify the address you want to delegate to.

---

## 4. How to Check and Withdraw Tokens from Vesting?

- Go to your personal dashboard at [lapida.one](https://lapida.one)
- Open the "Vesting" section.
- View unlocking status (start date, cliff, linear distribution).
- Click "Release" to withdraw available unlocked tokens to your wallet.

---

## 5. How to Use Lapida Tokens?

- **LPD:** payments for platform services, donations, store of value.
- **MLPD:** farming/staking, earning rewards, internal platform economics.
- **GLPD:** voting, proposal creation, project governance.

---

## 6. Security & Verification

- Always double-check token and contract addresses on the official website [lapida.one](https://lapida.one).
- Never share your private keys or seed phrases.
- Smart contracts are audited, source code is available on GitHub.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Which wallets are supported?**  
A: MetaMask, TrustWallet, Binance Chain Wallet, and any BNB Chain-compatible wallet.

**Q2: Where can I find token and contract addresses?**  
A: On the [lapida.one](https://lapida.one) website in the "Tokens & Contracts" section.

**Q3: What to do if tokens don’t arrive after purchase?**  
A: Check your transaction status on BSCscan. If unresolved — contact support via the form on the website.

**Q4: How does vesting work?**  
A: Your tokens unlock according to schedule; details are visible in your dashboard. Use the "Release" function to withdraw unlocked tokens.

**Q5: How do I join the DAO?**  
A: Simply hold GLPD tokens in your wallet and use the platform to vote.

**Q6: Can I sell tokens?**  
A: Yes, on PancakeSwap or other supported DEXs after they are unlocked.

**Q7: How do I learn about new proposals and events?**  
A: News is published on the website, Telegram channel, and via email newsletter.

**Q8: How to keep my assets safe?**  
A: Store private keys securely, use trusted wallets, avoid suspicious links.
