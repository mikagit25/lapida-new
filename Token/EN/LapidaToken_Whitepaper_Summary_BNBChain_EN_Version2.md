# Lapida Token — Whitepaper Summary (BNB Chain)

---

## 1. Project Vision

Lapida Token is a decentralized platform on the BNB Chain for memorial management, donations, and community governance.  
It combines stablecoin and utility tokens with DAO mechanisms and NFT memorials, aiming for transparency, security, and scalability.

---

## 2. Core Features

- **Multi-token architecture:** LPD (stablecoin), MLPD (utility), GLPD (governance)
- **DAO governance:** On-chain voting, proposal management, delegation
- **Memorial NFTs:** Digital memorial creation, management, and transfer
- **Secure platform:** Multisig, timelock, regular audits, open-source code
- **API & integrations:** REST/Web3 APIs for external platforms, wallets, DEX, and services

---

## 3. Technical Overview

- **BNB Chain base, BEP-20/721/1155 standards**
- **Smart contracts:** Modular, upgradeable, audited by SOLIDproof/OpenZeppelin
- **Reserve-backed stablecoin (LPD)**
- **Vesting contracts for team/investor allocations**
- **Open API/documentation for partners and developers**

---

## 4. Tokenomics

- LPD: Payment/donation stablecoin, reserve-backed, dynamic supply
- MLPD: Utility, farming/staking rewards, fixed supply
- GLPD: Governance, DAO voting, limited supply
- Transparent distribution, vesting, and DAO treasury management

---

## 5. Community & Partnerships

- Active social media: Telegram, Twitter/X, Medium, YouTube, Reddit
- Strategic partners: PinkSale, PancakeSwap, TofuNFT, OpenZeppelin, SOLIDproof
- Engagement: AMAs, airdrops, contests, bug bounties, ambassador programs

## 6. Roadmap Highlights

- Q1–Q2 2024: MVP, smart contracts, pre-sale, public launch
- Q3 2024: DEX/NFT integrations, staking, community campaigns, audits
- Q4 2024: DAO upgrades, mobile app, API release, advanced features
- 2025+: Multi-chain expansion, new partnerships, ongoing updates

---

## 7. Legal & Compliance

- Utility/governance token model, not a security
- KYC/AML for sales, GDPR for data
- Restrictions for certain jurisdictions
- Public audits, contract addresses, and legal contacts published
