# Lapida Token — Roadmap (BNB Chain)

---

## 1. Q1 2024 — Concept & MVP

- Project research, team formation
- Development of basic smart contracts (LPD, MLPD, GLPD)
- MVP of Lapida memorial platform and DAO
- First internal tests, security review

---

## 2. Q2 2024 — Pre-sale & Public Launch

- Private/seed rounds, onboarding initial partners
- IDO on PinkSale, public sale on PancakeSwap
- Listing of Lapida tokens (LPD, MLPD, GLPD)
- Launch of mainnet contracts (vesting, DAO, reserve)
- Open source publication on GitHub

---

## 3. Q3 2024 — Ecosystem Expansion

- Platform integrations: NFT memorials, DEX, wallets
- Launch of farming and staking programs (MLPD)
- Community growth, ambassador/influencer campaigns
- Bug bounty, first external audits (SOLIDproof, OpenZeppelin)

---

## 4. Q4 2024 — Advanced Features

- DAO upgrades: advanced voting, delegation, proposal factory
- Multisig/timelock implementation for reserves and critical functions
- API release for third-party integrations
- Launch of mobile app and partner services

---

## 5. 2025+ — Scaling & Global Expansion

- Expansion to new chains (Polygon, Ethereum, etc.)
- Partnerships with global memorial services and Web3 projects
- Continuous platform updates, new features based on community proposals
- Ongoing audits, compliance improvements

---

## 6. Roadmap Updates

- All roadmap changes are published on [lapida.one](https://lapida.one) and GitHub
- Community votes via DAO can affect roadmap priorities

---

*This roadmap is subject to change based on project progress, community input, and market conditions.*
